Good
questions.

Do I need to be fit before I start?

No. Your starting point shapes the plan. Whether you’re new to training, returning after a break or ready to move past a plateau, coaching starts with what you can do now.

Can I train at home?

Yes. Your programme is built around the equipment and time you have. A gym gives us more options, but it isn’t a requirement.

Will I have to give up the food I enjoy?

No. You’ll have calorie and protein targets, plus meal options that suit you. Meals out and social occasions are part of life. We work with that.

What support do I get between check-ins?

You can message Kalend through Everfit with questions, updates or videos for technique feedback. Your weekly review is the anchor, with support between reviews when you need guidance.

Is this online or in-person coaching?

This is online coaching across the UK. You train independently with a personal plan, feedback and regular reviews. If you need a trainer physically beside you for every session, an in-person PT may suit you better.

What happens after the first twelve weeks?

You choose whether to continue at £150 per month on a rolling basis or finish. There’s no new twelve-week commitment. The aim is progress and understanding you can keep.
